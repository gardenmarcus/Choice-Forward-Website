Workshop FAQ

How is a workshop different from a keynote?

A keynote inspires a full room. A workshop is hands-on: your team works through exercises together, with a workbook to keep.

Can we go deeper than one session?

Yes. Ask about the Choice Forward cohort or the wellness assessment.

Can it be paired with a keynote?

Yes. Many clients pair a morning keynote with an afternoon breakout workshop.
