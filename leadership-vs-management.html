Leadership vs. Management

Managing is about handling what happens. Leading is about choosing how you show up when it does.

Book this talk

About this talk

Marcus shows how to be the calm, proactive presence in hard exchanges, set boundaries that actually hold, and build communication and community a team can grow in.

This talk challenges the idea that management and leadership are the same. Participants learn where management helps with process and where leadership changes outcomes: in tone, trust, accountability, and culture.

With practical stories and clear language, Marcus equips leaders to respond with steadiness under pressure and to build teams that can adapt without burning out.

Your audience will leave with

  • A clear distinction between management tasks and leadership choices
  • Practical strategies for communication and boundary-setting
  • A framework for building resilient, connected team culture

Best for

Managers, leaders, customer-facing teams
